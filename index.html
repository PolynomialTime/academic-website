Bio

I obtained my PhD in computer science from the University of Auckland, after completing First Class Honours there in 2018. Before moving to New Zealand, I received my B.S. in computer science from Beijing Institute of Technology.

My research spans reinforcement learning, multi-agent systems and game theory. A central thread is reward learning from data, where I focus on both algorithmic design and applications. More recently, I have focused on LLMs, behaviour modelling, ethical AI, and responsible AI.

Outside research and teaching, I spend time hiking and making photographs. That visual sensibility matters to how I present research too: clarity, sequence, atmosphere, and strong composition. These photo collections are archived in my personal online gallery.
